Our Heritage

The Aurora Grand Story

Founded with a vision of blending classical grand hospitality with modern contemporary styling, Aurora Grand has served as an oasis of sophistication for over a decade. Every room, corridor, and landscape was intentionally designed to manifest a luxurious home away from home.

Located on the coastal cliffs overlooking the turquoise ocean, our hotel captures refreshing natural breezes during the day and hosts majestic reflecting sunset glows over our world-renowned infinity pool at night.

"We do not merely offer rooms; we curate unforgettable experiences, where luxury knows no bounds."

Whether you seek a relaxing wellness retreat in our holistic spa, a Michelin-star gastronomy tasting journey, or an exclusive villa with private ocean access, Aurora Grand is committed to fulfilling your highest standards of hospitality.

Our Mission

To provide an unparalleled luxury sanctuary that combines traditional five-star hospitality with modern innovation, creating unforgettable memories and delivering personalized care for every guest.

Our Vision

To be recognized globally as the ultimate standard of luxury hospitality, where architectural grandeur, natural tranquility, and meticulous, proactive service exist in perfect harmony.

Chronology

The Historic Journey

Explore the timeline of milestones and expansions that shaped Aurora Grand into a luxury hospitality landmark.

2011

Groundbreaking Ceremony

Purchased the exclusive coastal cliff acreage and broke ground for the primary structural foundations of the resort lobby.

2013

Grand Opening

Opened our doors to the public, offering 30 luxury deluxe rooms, a fine-dining dining room, and our central outdoor pool.

2018

Resort Expansion

Expanded our inventory by building the Executive and Presidential suites, along with launching our iconic cliffside infinity pool and luxury wellness spa.

2022

Michelin Accolades

Our signature dining room was awarded its first Michelin Star, establishing Aurora Grand as a premier gastronomic destination.

2026

Sustainable Villas

Launched the Honeymoon Villas featuring private plunge pools, and integrated fully carbon-neutral solar energy microgrids across the estate.
